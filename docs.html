Every page of the rutter docs, in the order most readers want them. To install rutter and reach a first recall, start at Get started.

  • Why is it built this way?: the argument for the design, a worked example, and what each choice costs.
  • How it works: memory-of-use is the record of how you used your notes. This page covers capture, recall, note identity, and positions in full mechanical detail.
  • FAQ: how several clients share one memory, adding a client to a working setup, double capture, and missing records.
  • From source: set up Claude Code from a clone of the repository. For contributors.
  • Pause, remove, and privacy: notice when capture stops, pause it, remove a record, and what leaves your machine.
  • Known issues: what is confirmed broken or untested, each with what you see, why, and what to do.
